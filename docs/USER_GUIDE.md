AquaPredict AI — User Guide

Purpose

This guide explains the intended workflow for exploring water scarcity information through AquaPredict AI.

Typical Workflow

1. Open the application.
2. Select a geographical region.
3. Review the available rainfall, groundwater, temperature, supply, and demand information.
4. Examine the displayed scarcity assessment.
5. Review the data source and date.
6. Explore potential water sources, if verified location data is available.
7. Compare possible conservation interventions.
8. Review the assumptions and limitations before acting on the results.

Understanding Water Deficit

A positive water deficit means the entered demand exceeds the entered supply. A negative value means the entered supply exceeds demand.

The calculation is only as reliable as its inputs.

Understanding Maps

Map markers identify recorded or demonstration locations. Their presence does not confirm that water is safe to drink, legally available, or suitable for a particular purpose.

Important Notice

AquaPredict AI is a decision-support prototype. Users should consult qualified authorities before making water-resource, public-health, or infrastructure decisions.
